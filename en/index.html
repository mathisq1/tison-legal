Tison privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tison is an app that helps a group of friends plan and enjoy a trip together: shared expenses, votes, dates, a document vault and a photo gallery. This page explains what data Tison processes, why, with whom, for how long, and how to delete it.

Who is responsible for your data

Tison is published by Mathis Querne (sole trader, micro-entrepreneur), 9 allée Nungesser et Coli, 93160 Noisy-le-Grand, France, reachable at contact.tison@proton.me. Write to this address with any question about your data.

What Tison does not do

  • No advertising, no audience measurement, no cross-app or cross-site tracking. The only technical exception, on Android: Google's text recognition module (ML Kit) sends Google usage statistics, without the content of your images (see "Autofill").
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No access to your location, microphone or contacts. The camera only opens if you choose "Take a photo" to read a receipt (see "Autofill").
  • No reading of your photo library without your permission, and no photo upload without your approval.

The data Tison processes

Your account

  • Email address and password: to create your account and sign in. The password is stored hashed, an irreversible process: no one, including us, can read it.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional, instead of email and password): Apple or Google confirms your identity to Tison and sends it your email address (with Apple, this may be a relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") and an account identifier specific to Tison. Your first name sent by Apple or Google becomes your display name, which you can change in My account. Google also sends your full name and the address of your Google profile photo: our host stores them with your account, but Tison does not display them or share them with anyone. Tison never receives your Apple or Google password.
  • Display name: so that the members of your trips know who suggested, paid for or voted for what.
  • Profile photo (optional): visible only to the members of your trips.
  • Technical sign-in data: for the security of the service, our host records the IP address and device type at sign-in.

What you add to a trip

Everything you add to a trip is visible to the members of that trip, and only them:

  • trip name, destination, dates, budget, cover photo;
  • your availability, your votes and your suggestions (destinations, stays, polls);
  • the expenses you enter, how they are split, repayments and your indicative budget. Tison processes no means of payment: these are simply amounts;
  • the documents and bookings in the vault (tickets, confirmations, Wi-Fi codes, gate code, key-safe code, address of the stay…);
  • the photos added to the gallery.
What you put in the vault. The whole group sees the content of the vault. Do not put identity documents, banking details or health data in it.
Photos. When you sync your photos (iPhone), Tison reads the photos taken during the trip dates in your photo library and suggests them to you; nothing is uploaded without your approval. Before uploading, Tison removes the location information (GPS coordinates) contained in the photos. The same applies to photos of stays, profile photos, cover photos and images put in the vault.

Photos showing other people. If you post a photo showing a person, make sure they agree. Anyone concerned can report the photo in the app or write to us to have it removed.

Autofill

Forms (stay, journey, booking, expense) can be prefilled from a screenshot, pasted text or a link. To do this, Tison sends Mistral AI, an artificial intelligence service, only:

  • the text read from the screenshot or receipt photo you choose. This text is read on your phone: the image is never sent, and the copy made for reading is deleted immediately. On iPhone, reading uses Vision, built into iOS, which sends nothing. On Android, it uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone but sends Google usage statistics (phone model and Android version, reading time and success, an identifier specific to the installation), never the image or the text read;
  • the text you paste (8,000 characters at most);
  • for a link, the public information on the page (title, description, displayed price…), summarized in 4,000 characters at most, along with the message that came with the link if it was shared to Tison.

This text may contain personal data (names, booking references). It is used only to fill in the form: you check and save the fields yourself. Neither Tison nor Mistral AI uses it for anything else, and Mistral AI does not use it to train its models.

Your permission is requested in the app before the first upload. Without it, nothing is sent and the form is filled in by hand. You can withdraw it at any time in My account › Privacy › AI autofill: you will be asked again the next time you use it. This choice is kept on your phone and erased when you sign out.

Tison keeps neither the text, the link nor the AI's answer: only the date, the type of form, the trip, the author and the result of the reading (successful or not) are recorded, to count each trip's autofills. For a link, Tison also keeps a fingerprint of the address (a code calculated from it), so as not to count the same link twice in a trip.

Listing previews. When you paste or share the link of a listing (Airbnb, Booking…), your phone opens the page itself to read its public information (title, photo, price). The listing's website therefore receives your phone's IP address, as if you opened it in a browser. The preview obtained (with no data about you) is kept for 7 days so the page doesn't have to be reloaded.

Purchases (Trip Pass)

If you buy the Trip Pass, payment is handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play: Tison never receives your card details. To credit your account, Tison and its provider RevenueCat process:

  • the technical identifier of your Tison account (never your email or your name);
  • your purchase history: plan purchased, price and currency, store country, date, transaction identifier, and any refund;
  • your credits: the trip on which a credit was used.

The trip on which you activate the Pass shows all its members "Trip Pass · offered by" followed by your first name.

Invitation link

Anyone with a trip's invitation link or code sees, without an account, on the link's page (tison.download), in the app and in the link preview shown by messaging apps: the trip's cover photo, its name, its dates, the number of travelers and the organizer's first name. No other first name, profile photo or other trip content appears. The page sets no cookies and uses no trackers.

Moderation and security

  • Reports: if you report a photo or a participant, we record your report (reason, optional comment, content concerned) and the moderation team is notified by email. As long as your report exists, the reported photo is hidden for you.
  • Blocks: the list of people you have blocked.
  • Invitation codes: when an invalid invitation code is entered or opened (in the app or on the invitation link page), we record the account and IP address concerned to limit abusive attempts.

Notifications

Tison shows notifications in the app (new photo, dates confirmed…). They are stored with their read / unread status.

Push notifications (only if you allow them on your phone): Tison records a technical identifier of your device (notification token) so it can send them to you. The text of a notification (trip name, member's first name, name of the expense or stay…) passes through Expo's notification service, then through Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android), which display it on your phone.

Notification settings: your pace (instant or grouped), the daily summary and its time, quiet hours and the categories you want to receive. To send the summary and respect quiet hours at your local time, Tison also records your phone's time zone (for example "Europe/Paris"); it is updated when your phone changes time zone. To write to you in your language, Tison also records the language of the app on your phone (French or English). To space out sends, Tison notes the date of each grouped notification sent, without its content.

Why we process this data (legal bases)

  • Performance of the service you requested by creating an account (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, trips, expenses, votes, vault, gallery, photo sync, notifications, form autofill (after your permission in the app), crediting the Trip Pass you buy.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): keeping proof of sale (plan, price, date, transaction identifier).
  • Legitimate interest in securing the service and protecting it from abuse (Article 6(1)(f)): limiting invitation code attempts, reports, blocks, sign-in logs.

Access to your photo library also requires a permission on your phone, which you can withdraw at any time in its settings.

Who this data is shared with

  • The members of your trips, for what you add to those trips.
  • Our technical providers, who process data on our behalf only:
    • Supabase (database, files, authentication and account emails: sign-up confirmation, password reset), servers located in the European Union (Ireland);
    • Cloudflare (hosting of the invitation link page, tison.download: receives the IP address and browser of page visitors, and sends it the trip preview described in "Invitation link");
    • RevenueCat (management of Trip Pass in-app purchases: technical account identifier and purchase history, see "Purchases");
    • Resend (sending the moderation email for each report: reason, comment and technical identifiers of the content concerned; and the email that warns before an inactive account is deleted: your email address and display name);
    • Expo (sending push notifications: device token and notification text, see "Notifications"), which hands them to Apple or Google for display;
    • Mistral AI (France), the artificial intelligence service used for autofill: screenshot text, pasted text or public link information, see "Autofill". Mistral AI does not use this data for training and keeps it only for the time needed to read it, or at most for the abuse-monitoring period set out in its data processing agreement;
    • Google (ML Kit, Android only): usage statistics for reading screenshot text, without the image or the text, see "Autofill".
  • Freely accessible third-party services queried by the app, which receive your device's IP address like any website, but no data from your account:
    • Open-Meteo, which receives the coordinates of suggested destinations to show their weather;
    • Photon (komoot), which receives the place name you type in the destination search;
    • Pexels, which provides illustration photos of destinations (the search is made by our servers with the destination name only; your device then downloads the image);
    • listing websites whose link you paste or share (Airbnb, Booking…), opened by your phone to show their preview.
  • Apple and Google, which collect payment for the Trip Pass and, if you choose them to sign in, confirm your identity, under their own terms and privacy policies. They then know that you use Tison; Tison sends them no data about your trips.
  • The authorities, only if the law requires us to.

Transfers outside the European Union

Supabase hosts your data in the European Union (Ireland). Mistral AI, which reads the autofill text, is a French company that processes this data in the European Union.

A few providers are American companies: Cloudflare (invitation link page), RevenueCat (Trip Pass purchases), Expo (650 Industries, delivery of push notifications), Resend (moderation and inactive-account warning emails) and Google (ML Kit statistics on Android). These transfers are governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses included in their data processing agreements. You can ask us for a copy at contact.tison@proton.me.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: as long as you use it. An account unused for 2 years (no signed-in opening of the app) is deleted, just as if you deleted it yourself (see "Deleting your account"). An email warns you 30 days before: just sign in to keep it.
  • Trip content: as long as the trip exists. A finished trip remains viewable, read-only, by its members.
  • Notifications: as long as the trip they relate to exists.
  • Notification settings, time zone and language: as long as your account exists. Notification token: until the device signs out, the permission is withdrawn or the account is deleted. Dates of grouped notifications sent: 7 days.
  • Autofill: the text sent is not kept by Tison; the date, type of form, trip, author and result of a counted reading are kept as long as the trip exists (a reading that isn't counted: one day).
  • Invitation code attempts (account and IP address): one day.
  • Reports: 6 months after they are handled, then deleted automatically.
  • Trip Pass credits: an unused credit has no expiry date: it is kept as long as your account exists. An activated Pass remains attached to the trip as long as the trip exists.
  • Purchase history: 10 years, the legal retention period for accounting records (Article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code). After your account is deleted, it is kept with no link to it.
  • Sign-in log (email address, IP address, date and type of event: sign-up, sign-in, password change): 6 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Host's technical logs (requests received by its servers): according to Supabase's own retention policy.
  • On your phone: your trip data kept for offline display is erased when you sign out. Only expenses entered offline and not yet sent are kept until they are sent, the next time you are online.

Deleting your account

In the app: My account › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent:

  • your account, your email address, your profile and your profile photo are deleted, as well as the information received from Apple or Google if you signed in with them;
  • if you signed in with Apple, Tison asks you for a final Apple confirmation and revokes its access: Tison disappears from the list of apps using your Apple ID. With Google, you can remove Tison's access in your Google account settings (Security › Your connections to third-party apps & services);
  • a trip of which you are the only member is deleted with all its content;
  • in a shared trip, you now appear as "Deleted member". Your expenses and repayments are kept under this name, so the group's accounts remain correct. Your votes and suggestions are kept in the same way. Your gallery photos and vault documents are deleted. The photos of a stay you suggested and a trip's cover photo remain, because they belong to the group's trip, with no link to your identity;
  • if you created a shared trip, the organizer role passes to the longest-standing member;
  • your unused Trip Pass credits are lost. A Pass already activated remains with the trip's group, without your name. Proof of sale is kept with no link to your account.

You can also request deletion without using the app, at contact.tison@proton.me or via the Account deletion page.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access your data, rectify it, erase it, request its portability, object to certain processing or request its restriction, by writing to contact.tison@proton.me. We reply within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country of residence.

Security

All exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS), and data is encrypted on our host's servers. Your session is kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). The access rules of the database and files restrict each piece of data to the members of the trip concerned.

In the event of a data breach likely to present a risk to you, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, we inform you directly.

Minimum age

Tison is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes significantly, we will let you know in the app before the changes take effect.

Report. Any member can report a photo or a participant from the app. Anyone, even without an account, can also report content to contact.tison@proton.me. We review each report within 24 hours and may remove the content, suspend or delete the account concerned.

Block. Any member can block another participant: their photos are then hidden, but only for you — the rest of the group still sees them. The blocked person is not notified and you both remain members of the shared trips. You can unblock at any time.

Decisions. If we remove one of your contents or suspend your account, we inform you by email stating the reason, unless the law prohibits us from doing so. You can challenge the decision by replying to that email; we will then review the decision again.

Serious abuse. In the event of serious abuse (sexual content involving a minor, threats, clear harassment), the account may be deleted immediately, without prior warning.

Reports are kept for 6 months after they are handled, then deleted automatically.

7. Trip Pass (in-app purchase)

The Trip Pass is an optional, one-time purchase (not a subscription). It comes in the form of credits: one credit activates the Pass on a trip of up to 10 people, two credits on a trip of up to 30 people. The Pass's perks, its price and the number of credits in each plan are shown in the app before purchase.

Payment. The purchase is made and paid for through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play, under their terms. Tison receives no card details.

Credits. Credits are linked to your account and have no expiry date. A credit used on a trip remains attached to it, for the benefit of the whole group, as long as the trip exists, even if you leave the trip or delete your account. Unused credits are lost when your account is deleted.

Right of withdrawal. The Pass is digital content supplied as soon as the purchase is complete. By confirming the purchase, you ask to benefit from it immediately and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal (Article L221-28, 13° of the French Consumer Code).

Refunds. Refund requests are made to Apple or Google, under their terms. If a purchase is refunded, the corresponding credits are removed; a Pass activated with those credits may then be suspended.

Account deletion — Tison

How to delete your account

From the app (recommended)

  1. Open Tison and go to My account › Delete my account.
  2. Read the summary of what will be deleted and kept.
  3. Type "DELETE" to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent, and you are signed out on all your devices.

Without the app

Write to contact.tison@proton.me from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Account deletion". We may ask you for confirmation to check that the request really comes from you. Your account is deleted within one month at most, and we confirm the deletion by email.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write to us anyway explaining the situation: we will ask you for information to check that the account belongs to you.

Unused account

An account unused for 2 years (no signed-in opening of the app) is deleted automatically, in the same way. An email warns 30 days before: just sign in to keep the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, display name and profile photo, as well as the information received from Apple or Google if you signed in with them.
  • Tison's access to your Apple ID, if you signed in with Apple: it is revoked after a final Apple confirmation. For a Google account, you can remove Tison's access in your Google account settings.
  • Your gallery photos and vault documents, including in shared trips.
  • Your availability, your blocks, your notifications and your settings.
  • On your phone: your trip data kept for offline display.
  • Any trip of which you are the only member, with all its content (expenses, votes, documents, photos).

What is kept, and for how long

  • In a shared trip, your expenses, repayments, votes and suggestions remain visible to the other members under the name "Deleted member", with no link to your identity, so the group's accounts remain correct. They are kept as long as the trip exists.
  • The photos of a stay you suggested and the cover photo of a shared trip remain in the trip, with no link to your identity: they belong to the group's trip.
  • If you organized a shared trip, the organizer role passes to the trip's longest-standing member.
  • Your Trip Pass purchases: proof of sale is kept for 10 years (accounting obligation), with no link to your account. Your unused credits are lost.
  • Reports you made or that concerned you are kept for 6 months after they are handled, for the security of the service, then deleted automatically.
  • Our host's backups may contain your data for a limited time after deletion, according to Supabase's own backup policy.
  • The sign-in log (email address, IP address, sign-in dates) is kept for 6 months, then deleted automatically.
