How to delete your account

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · English translation, the French version prevails

From the app (recommended)

  1. Open Tison and go to My account › Delete my account.
  2. Read the summary of what will be deleted and kept.
  3. Type "DELETE" to confirm.
Deletion is immediate and permanent. You are signed out on all your devices and the account cannot be recovered afterwards.

Without the app

Write to contact.tison@proton.me from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Account deletion". We may ask you for confirmation to check that the request really comes from you. Your account is deleted within one month at most, and we confirm the deletion by email.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write to us anyway explaining the situation: we will ask you for information to check that the account belongs to you.

Request deletion by email

Unused account

An account unused for 2 years (no signed-in opening of the app) is deleted automatically, in the same way. An email warns 30 days before: just sign in to keep the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, display name and profile photo, as well as the information received from Apple or Google if you signed in with them.
  • Tison's access to your Apple ID, if you signed in with Apple: it is revoked after a final Apple confirmation. For a Google account, you can remove Tison's access in your Google account settings.
  • Your gallery photos and vault documents, including in shared trips.
  • Your availability, your blocks, your notifications and your settings.
  • On your phone: your trip data kept for offline display.
  • Any trip of which you are the only member, with all its content (expenses, votes, documents, photos).

What is kept, and for how long

  • In a shared trip, your expenses, repayments, votes and suggestions remain visible to the other members under the name "Deleted member", with no link to your identity, so the group's accounts remain correct. They are kept as long as the trip exists.
  • The photos of a stay you suggested and the cover photo of a shared trip remain in the trip, with no link to your identity: they belong to the group's trip.
  • If you organized a shared trip, the organizer role passes to the trip's longest-standing member.
  • Your Trip Pass purchases: proof of sale is kept for 10 years (accounting obligation), with no link to your account. Your unused credits are lost.
  • Reports you made or that concerned you are kept for 6 months after they are handled, for the security of the service, then deleted automatically.
  • Our host's backups may contain your data for a limited time after deletion, according to Supabase's own backup policy.
  • The sign-in log (email address, IP address, sign-in dates) is kept for 6 months, then deleted automatically.
